# Licensing & where to get a licence

**Flink Manager is commercial, licensed software.** It is **not open source and not free**.
Using it in any environment (development, staging, or production) requires a valid paid
commercial licence issued by **Vinteros Labs**.

## How to obtain a licence

> 🌐 **To purchase / apply for a licence, visit the company website: [https://vinteros-labs.com/](https://vinteros-labs.com/)**

1. Go to **[vinteros-labs.com](https://vinteros-labs.com/)**.
2. Find the **Flink Manager** product / licensing section.
3. For a quick evaluation, grab a **free trial licence** directly at
   **[vinteros-labs.com/products/flink-manager/license/](https://vinteros-labs.com/products/flink-manager/license/)** —
   the website issues a testing licence instantly, and Vinteros Labs keeps it renewed
   automatically.
4. For a production licence, request one via the enquiry form or contact details shown on the
   website; the Vinteros Labs team will respond with terms, pricing, and the licence you need.

*(Exact URL paths and contact email may change — refer to the live website.)*

## What the documentation assumes

Throughout this documentation the term **"licence"** refers to the commercial licence issued by
Vinteros Labs. You should have your licence (or a valid evaluation entitlement) **before**
deploying Flink Manager.

## Unlicensed use

Running Flink Manager without a valid licence is not permitted. If you do not yet hold a
licence, contact Vinteros Labs through the website before installation.
